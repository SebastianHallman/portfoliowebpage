For the past 8 weeks, I have been going to rehab 2 days a week to have focused days where I exercise in areas that my body needs. I was recommended by my nurse who meets me regularly for my baclofen pump adjustments to go to a rehabilitation center to keep my spasticity under control. This is something you can get prescribed by your doctor once a year and is a great way to reduce the everyday spasticity that you feel. And in the beginning, they said that you do this once a year and then not again until the next year. This is apparently the best way to keep your spasticity under control, but after these weeks, I feel that I need to continue, and the personnel there agree.

## The experience

I went to Aleris Rehab Station in Stockholm, which is a place for both daily rehab and more long-term rehab for people with spinal cord injuries. The first week, I did do some tests and met my own team that I was going to work with. I was assigned a physical therapist and an occupational therapist, and they figured out what I needed to work on based on my goals, which were mainly endurance and coordination. I mean, strength is nice, but when I do not have good coordination, I cannot really gain much from getting stronger. Based on that, I was assigned both individual and group sessions, and both were nice.

One of the best sessions I had was when I did some exercise in the Lokomat. The Lokomat is an exo-skeleton-like machine that makes you walk in the right way. It is a passive training machine that does the movement for you. It might seem to be a relaxing experience, but it does require energy from you. You can also move by yourself too, but it can be a negative if you do not do the right movements, which is rough with spasticity. It was definitely one of the better sessions I had if we disregard that the security measures did cause some pain, but it was part of the exercise and could not be avoided.

## The gym

I have previously gone to a regular gym, but it was not very well optimized for me. I could use like 5 machines and that was it. And I could not reach some of the machines. At the rehab center, their gym was big and spacious. Maybe it was fewer machines overall, but I could use everything. They even had an arm-bicycle, which is my new favorite. I used it every day I had rehab because I had an endurance group and that was a great way to end the day. I kind of want one at home now. Too bad they are quite expensive, as accessibility items always are. Praise the disability tax!

## My plan going forward

Despite my rehab sessions being over, I will continue to visit the rehab center. They have a gym card that gives you access to the gym during lunchtime. Despite the time limitation and the commute time, I find it definitely worth it to get their gym card and go there twice a week. It is fun and I haven't felt this good in a while, both physically and mentally. I am happy that I had these 8 weeks and I am ready to continue maintaining my body as I do with my programming projects.
