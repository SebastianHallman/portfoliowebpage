Mathematics is already a very difficult subject, and many people struggle with it. But what if solving the math was the easy part, and the hard part was writing the solution down? That is what I struggled with as a student. I can't use pen and paper because of my limited motor skills, so I have tried every way of working with math digitally. From plain text editors to writing LaTeX during exams, I have tried it all. LaTeX works, but I should not have to focus on its syntax while I am trying to solve the problem. This is where myMathBook comes in.

## A math editor that understands what you want

At its core, myMathBook is a lot like Google Docs, with quality-of-life improvements for writing math. The key feature is a regex parser that recognizes math. For example, if you write 1/2=x^2+5x+1, the parser recognizes the equation, formats it as LaTeX, and renders it in real time. Write the math and press space, and you get it formatted the way you would on paper.

![Plain text becomes a rendered equation when you press space](/img/mymathbook-text-latex.gif)

## Different tools for different tasks

Math is not just about writing the solution down. Sometimes you need to visualize it, build a table, or do column calculations by hand. myMathBook takes inspiration from Notion and uses blocks, so you can mix those tools in one document.

Want to sketch a graph? Use the diagram block to draw it, or describe it with a short script.

![The diagram block plots a function from a short script, or you can draw it by hand](/img/mymathbook-graph.gif)

Need a table? Use the table block. Want to work through a calculation column by column? Use the grid block.

![The grid block is a sheet of cells for column calculations](/img/mymathbook-grid.gif)

Need to lay the solution out the way you would on paper, instead of being stuck with the left-to-right flow of a normal text editor? Use the layout block. That is the core of myMathBook: your thoughts, your rules.

## Current status of myMathBook

I work on myMathBook whenever I get an idea for it. It is a passion project, and I will keep improving it for as long as I can. There is no plan for a proper release, but anyone can try it with a free account and 10 documents. If you use it regularly and need more documents, contact me and I will raise the limit.
